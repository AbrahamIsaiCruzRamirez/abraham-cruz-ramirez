Biography

Born in San Francisco and raised in San Antonio, Abraham began singing at 15 at Thomas Edison High School — joining choir to stay eligible for sports. Under the mentorship of Valerie Watson, his talent earned him a spot in the Texas All-State Mixed Choir (2017).

His recent debut with Austin Opera as Benoît & Alcindoro in La Bohème at the Long Center marked a significant milestone in his emerging career. A studio artist with Aspen Opera Theater and VocalARTS, he holds first-place honors from NATS, the National Society of Arts and Letters, Opera Modesto, and the Classical Singer Competition. Selected as a 2026 Lehrer Vocal Institute Fellow at the Music Academy of the West.
